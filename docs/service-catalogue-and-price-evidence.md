## Pricing

The [published pricing page](https://8westit.com/pricing.html) covers the **8 West IT 365 software suite for MSPs**, priced per technician. Those are not managed IT support prices for local small businesses.

Use **consultation and a custom quote after discovery**. Do not promise a free consultation, price range, discount, minimum term, currency, tax treatment, response time, after-hours support, on-site travel or Spanish-language service delivery without confirmation.

A proposal may be drafted with unknowns labelled. Before it is approved for a customer, resolve:

- Service scope, supported people/devices/sites and exclusions.
- Price, currency, billing frequency, tax and payment terms.
- Hours, response commitments, after-hours and on-site arrangements.
- Language, contracting entity and invoicing requirements, especially for Mexico.
- Start date, onboarding work and who accepts the service handoff.
