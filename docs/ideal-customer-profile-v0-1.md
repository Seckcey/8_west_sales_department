# Ideal customer profile

## Confirmed

Local small businesses in San Diego, Los Angeles and Baja California, Mexico. In the Paperclip owner response on 2026-09-25, Frankie selected no industry preference. Employee size remains undecided; do not exclude a business solely because its headcount is unknown.

## Research priorities

Prioritize businesses with public professional contact information and a clear connection to the service area. Look for directly evidenced needs related to Microsoft 365, staff IT support, Wi-Fi/networking, account administration or security basics.

A website using Microsoft technology is a clue, not proof of budget, dissatisfaction or buying intent. Hiring, expansion and multiple sites can guide questions but must not be described as confirmed IT problems.

## Qualification

Quinn records four dimensions: territory fit, service fit, identifiable professional contact route and evidence of a relevant trigger. Each is supported, unknown or conflicting. Record the source and date. Do not infer an individual's sensitive traits, private finances or personal circumstances.

- **Ready for discovery:** relevant business, professional contact route and plausible service fit; remaining questions are explicit.
- **Needs research:** key facts or contact provenance are missing.
- **Outside scope:** evidenced mismatch with the territory or supported services.
- **Suppressed:** opt-out or another stop instruction; this overrides every score.

Budget, authority, timing, employee count and current provider are discovery questions unless a reliable source establishes them. The absence of a public budget is not a reason to invent one.
